Who We Are

About Ndhiwa CDF

Constituency Development Fund – Ndhiwa Constituency

The Constituency Development Fund is established under the NG-CDF Act of 2015, aimed at ensuring fair distribution of public resources at constituency level across Kenya.

Ndhiwa Constituency CDF Committee identifies, prioritizes, implements, and monitors development projects within the constituency and administers bursaries for needy students across all 7 wards.

Our Core Mandate

  • 🏗️Finance and oversee community infrastructure projects
  • 🎓Award bursaries to needy but deserving students
  • 🌱Support community-driven livelihood initiatives
  • 📊Ensure transparency and accountability in fund use
  • 🗣️Facilitate public participation in project selection
